About This Guide

The Concept

A video documentary series filmed across fifteen European countries. In each location, the same dish is prepared: Ragù alla Bolognese, following the official recipe deposited by the Accademia Italiana della Cucina at the Bologna Chamber of Commerce on 20 April 2023. The canonical recipe is the reference point. Everything else is a variation on that foundation.

The One Rule

Every chapter contains one locally-sourced, culturally specific ingredient not in the canonical recipe. It must integrate into the cooking process — not be used as garnish. It must change when or how something is cooked, not simply what is served alongside. It must be procurable at a named market in that country. It must be defensible on flavour grounds.

On Pasta

The 2023 Accademia recipe specifies fresh egg tagliatelle. Not spaghetti. Not linguine. Not penne. Every chapter in this guide uses tagliatelle or the closest local equivalent. Where a genuine local pasta substitution exists, it is noted. Where it doesn't, tagliatelle is non-negotiable.

Pricing Standards

All ingredient prices are approximate retail estimates as of early 2026. Costs appear in both local currency and USD equivalent. Exchange rates: EUR/USD 1.095 · GBP/USD 1.275 · SEK/USD 0.0928 · PLN/USD 0.254 · HUF/USD 0.00274 · RON/USD 0.220 · CZK/USD 0.0439.

The Canonical Recipe

Ingredients — Serves 6
Coarsely ground beef (cartella / pancia / fesone)400g
Fresh pork pancetta — not smoked, not cured150g
Strained tomatoes (passata)200g
Double-concentrate tomato paste1 tbsp
Onion (peeled)~60g
Carrot (peeled)~60g
Celery stalk (trimmed)~60g
Red or white wine (dry)~100ml
Whole milk (optional)~100ml
Extra virgin olive oil3 tbsp
Light broth or stock cubeas needed
Salt and pepperto taste
✓ Permitted (2023)

White or red wine. Olive oil or butter (50g). Fresh tagliatelle.

✗ Not Permitted (2023)

Cream. Smoked pancetta. Garlic. Herbs. Chicken livers.

The 2023 recipe's most important change: tomato reduced from 300g to ~250g effective, beef increased from 300g to 400g. The ratio shifted from 1:1 to ~1:2 (tomato:meat). This is a philosophical statement: this is a meat sauce with tomato — not a tomato sauce with meat. Every recipe in this guide respects that as its floor.

Method
1
Finely dice pancetta; render slowly in a heavy 24–26cm pan with EVOO over medium-low heat until fat runs and pieces are pale gold.
2
Add finely chopped onion, carrot, and celery. Sweat gently until soft — 8–10 minutes.
3
Add ground beef. Stir over raised heat until the meat sizzles and sputters — the Bolognese "sfrigola."
4
Pour in wine. Stir gently until fully evaporated — 3–4 minutes.
5
Add strained tomatoes and tomato paste. Cover partially and simmer on the lowest possible heat for a minimum of 2 hours, adding warm broth ladle by ladle as needed.
6
Toward the end, stir in whole milk to temper the acidity of the tomato. Adjust salt and pepper.
7
Serve with fresh egg tagliatelle. Never spaghetti.
